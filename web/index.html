How it works

  1. Plant

    Pick a duration, a species, and a tag, then press Plant & focus.

  2. Focus

    Blocked sites redirect to a reminder, and your plant keeps growing.

  3. Grow

    When the timer ends, the plant joins your grove. Keep going to earn rare species.

Chrome extension

Get the timer in your toolbar, with real site blocking. While the extension is in development, install it by: opening chrome://extensions, turning on Developer mode, choosing Load unpacked, and selecting the extension/ folder.
