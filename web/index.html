Install the extension

Install the Chrome extension

Grove isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you install it by hand. It takes about 2 minutes, and you only do it once.

  1. Download Grove

    Click the button to download a ZIP file.

    ⬇ Download Grove extension (.zip)

  2. Unzip it

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click grove-extension.zip. A folder called grove-extension appears.

    On Windows, right-click the file and choose Extract All…, then Extract.

    Move the folder somewhere you'll keep it, such as Documents. Chrome loads Grove from this folder, so don't delete it.

  3. Open the extensions page

    Copy this address, paste it into Chrome's address bar, and press Enter:

    chrome://extensions

    Using Edge? Use edge://extensions. Brave: brave://extensions.

  4. Turn on Developer mode

    Find the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner of the page and turn it on.

    In Edge, the switch is in the left sidebar. Developer mode just lets you add extensions from a folder on your computer.

  5. Load Grove

    Click Load unpacked (top-left), select the grove-extension folder you unzipped, and click Select.

    Pick the folder itself, not the .zip file. If Chrome says "Manifest file is missing", you picked the wrong folder. Open it and make sure it contains a file named manifest.json.

  6. Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 at the top-right of Chrome, then click the pin 📌 next to Grove. A 🌱 now appears in your toolbar. Click it to start focusing.

Updating to a new version: download the ZIP again, unzip it, and replace your old grove-extension folder with the new one. Then open chrome://extensions and click the ↻ reload icon on Grove's card. Your garden is kept.

How to use Grove

  1. Plant

    Click 🌱 in your toolbar (or open the web app). Drag the slider to pick a time, choose a plant and a tag, then press Plant & focus.

  2. Focus

    Get to work. If you open a blocked site, Grove shows your growing plant and the time left instead. Press Back to focus to return.

  3. Grow

    When the timer ends, your plant is fully grown and joins your garden. Hit milestones to earn rare species.

What makes a plant wither?

🌐 In the web app

Switching to another tab or app for more than 10 seconds, or pressing Give up. You can change the 10 seconds in Settings.

🧩 In the extension

Only pressing Give up. Other tabs are fine, but blocked sites stay blocked until your plant is grown.

Your data stays with you

Grove has no accounts, servers, ads or tracking. Your sessions and garden are saved only in your own browser. The extension uses its permissions only to block the sites you choose during a focus session. It never reads or sends your browsing anywhere.

Questions

Is Grove free?

Yes, completely. There's no paid version, no ads and no account.

Why isn't it in the Chrome Web Store?

It isn't yet. Until it is, the install steps above add it directly from the downloaded folder. It's the same extension either way.

Chrome says the extension needs to "read and change all your data on all websites". Why?

Chrome shows that warning for any extension that can block websites, because it can't know in advance which sites you'll block. Grove only uses this to redirect the sites on your block list during a focus session. It doesn't read pages, record your history or send anything anywhere.

Chrome shows a "Disable developer mode extensions" message. Is something wrong?

No. Chrome shows this reminder for every extension installed from a folder rather than the store. You can close it. Don't click Disable, or Grove will turn off.

How do I change which sites are blocked?

Click 🌱 in your toolbar, then Open garden, stats & collection →. Go to ⚙️ Settings, edit the list of blocked sites (one per line, for example youtube.com), and click Save.

Does it work on my phone?

The web app works in any phone browser. Keep the page open while you focus, because switching apps for more than 10 seconds withers your plant. The extension works only in desktop browsers.

Does it work in Safari or Firefox?

The web app does. The extension works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera). Firefox support is planned.

Will I lose my garden if I clear my browser data?

Yes. Your garden lives in your browser, so clearing site data, or removing the extension, deletes it. Back it up from ⚙️ Settings → Export data, and bring it back with Import data.

Can I use the web app and the extension together?

Yes, but each keeps its own garden. To move your garden from one to the other, export it from one and import it into the other.

How do I uninstall the extension?

Open chrome://extensions, find Grove and click Remove. You can then delete the grove-extension folder.
